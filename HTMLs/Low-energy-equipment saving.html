📚 Data Sources & Reference Guide

The data, figures, and examples in this comparison are drawn from the following authoritative sources. All energy savings estimates are illustrative calculations based on published efficiency benchmarks and typical usage assumptions.

Calculation Methodology & Assumptions

  • Electricity unit rate used throughout: 20p/kWh — representative of UK commercial tariffs (2023–2024 mid-range)
  • Water rate used: £0.004/litre — average UK commercial water & sewerage rate
  • CO₂ conversion factor: 0.21233 kg CO₂/kWh — UK National Grid electricity carbon intensity (DESNZ 2023)
  • Operating days: 250 days/year for ovens; 365 days/year for refrigeration
  • Combi oven cycles: 5 cycles/day × 250 days = 1,875 cycles/year
  • Undercounter dishwasher: 40 racks/day; hood-type: 80 racks/day (typical busy service)
  • Savings percentages are illustrative comparisons of energy-efficient models vs representative standard equivalents

✅ Action Plan

How to Protect Yourself From Price Volatility

The earlier you act, the more protected you are when the next price spike arrives. Here's a practical framework for any premises type.

01

Monitor Energy Prices Regularly

Subscribe to Ofgem updates and Eurostat energy data. Knowing when prices are rising helps you time investments and spot the best moments to act.

02

Calculate Your Actual Consumption

Sub-meter key areas (kitchen, lighting, HVAC). You can't manage what you don't measure. See Carbon Trust guidance on sub-metering.

03

Replace equipment with ETL-listed products

When equipment needs replacing, choose from the UK Energy Technology List — use official category names (e.g. refrigeration equipment, PFSE, heat pumps) to find top-quartile options.

04

Install Solar PV and Batteries

Generate your own electricity during daylight hours. Pair with a battery for evening coverage. A 4 kWp home system can cover most daytime needs; export via SEG where eligible.

05

Add Water-Saving Devices

Low-flow taps, spray valves and solar thermal heating reduce both water bills and the energy needed to heat that water — see Waterwise and Greenways Marketplace.

06

Review and Repeat Annually

Run the calculation every year against the latest prices. As you reduce consumption, your risk exposure falls — and your ROI on upgrades improves.

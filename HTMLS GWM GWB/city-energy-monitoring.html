Chapter 02 · Investor thesis

Why now: policy forces the meter — we own the insight

Smart metering is no longer optional in the Netherlands. The Energy Act mandate, EU coverage targets, and stacked provincial–national subsidies create a rare window: regulation installs the data pipe; Greenways turns that pipe into measurable hospitality savings — with public funding aligned to the same rollout.

Mandate
Legal deadline, not a trend

From 1 Jan 2026 the Energiewet requires replacement of obsolete analog meters. Net operators contact remaining sites through 2026–27; all must be digital by end-2027 — households and restaurants alike.

Alignment
Same agenda as government

Ministry of EZK, ACM, RDI and netbeheerders are finishing NTA 8130 smart meters. Investors fund a platform that sits on top of infrastructure the state is already forcing into every connection.

Capital efficiency
Subsidies co-fund the stack

North Holland smart-metering grant (up to 75% / €10k), ISDE, municipal horeca schemes, EIA and Flex-e can underwrite hardware, dashboards and efficiency measures — reducing private capital intensity at rollout.

Netherlands — mandatory replacement window

The remaining ~10% of analog points are now under a hard legal finish line. Refusal after reminders can trigger administrative fines (up to €1,650) via the new digital infrastructure inspectorate (RDI).

Europe — smart metering is settled policy

EU Directive 2019/944 set an 80% smart-meter ambition where cost-effective. The Netherlands sits in the “dynamic movers” group (~90% by 2024) — finishing the last analog sites under national law, not starting from scratch.

Investor read-across: markets that finish mandatory metering create demand for interpretation layers — submetering, KPIs, alerts, grant evidence — exactly the Greenways hospitality stack. Early-adopter countries are already moving to second-generation AMS; NL is forcing first-gen completion now.

Restaurants are in scope — and data is the product

The law targets obsolete meters at connection points, not “households only.” Cafés and restaurants still on analog are being scheduled like any other small business. For energy efficiency, the smart main meter is necessary but not sufficient — investors need the layer that turns 15-minute totals into kitchen, HVAC and FOH accountability.

Funding routes that reward metering & data-driven efficiency

Public programmes already pay for the monitoring layer and for the equipment it unlocks. At portfolio scale this is not a footnote — it is a structural reduction in go-to-market cost and a signal that product design matches Dutch and EU industrial policy.

North Holland · flagship for Amsterdam
SMMESNH — smart metering & monitoring
Up to 75%
of costs · max €10,000 · €300k pool · first-come

Covers purchase/rental of electricity smart metering & monitoring systems plus advice — insight into consumption, not appliance swap-outs. Open 31 Mar–1 Oct 2026; voucher route €700–€1,500 by 4 Dec 2026. Ideal for submeters, gateways, dashboard licences and KPI advisory.

National · RVO
ISDE — energy-saving measures
Performance-based
apply before purchase · NL premises

LED, insulation, efficient HVAC and selected appliances. Smart + submeter data strengthens ex-ante savings estimates and ex-post verification — the evidence municipalities and auditors expect.

Municipal · horeca / retail
Energy scan + implementation
Up to 50%
per measure · often max ~€1,500

Free or subsidised durability scan for hospitality; implementation grants for LED, insulation and sustainable equipment. Scans routinely recommend submetering — feeding both the Greenways install and follow-on subsidy files.

Tax · Energie-lijst
EIA — energy investment deduction
40% deduction
of qualifying investment from taxable profit

Advanced metering / EMS may qualify when listed; efficient assets monitored on-site often do. Stackable with ISDE or municipal aid where rules allow — improving after-tax IRR on portfolio upgrades.

Grid congestion · large consumers
Flex-e — flexibility scan & measures
RVO window 6 May – 15 Oct 2026
≥100 kW or >3×80 A with measurement contract · congestion areas

For restaurant groups, central kitchens and multi-site operators: flexibility studies and load-shifting / smart-control investments. Submetering and controls are typically core to eligible measures — a natural upsell once Greenways is embedded.

Investor takeaway

Regulation is finishing the metering layer across Dutch hospitality. Subsidies explicitly pay for monitoring and for the efficiency it unlocks. Greenways is positioned where policy, public capital and restaurant economics intersect — turning a mandatory meter into a funded, measurable transition product that scales city-block by city-block.

Recycle with Droppie

A step-by-step plan for restaurants and SMEs: choose what you are retiring, find the nearest Droppie store, prep for drop-off, and open the app. You can also send a heads-up for bulk or awkward equipment.

Step 1

What are you recycling?

Pick everything you plan to drop off. We will match stores that accept those streams.

Step 2

Nearest Droppie for your site

We estimate distance from your site. Adjust city or postcode if needed.

Directions

Select at least one stream in step 1 to see matching stores.

Step 3

Prep & open the Droppie app

At the store, staff scan your personal QR code in the app and register each stream.

  • Download the Droppie app and create your account before you travel.
  • Separate streams cleanly — sealed bags or containers, as clean as possible.
  • Check opening hours in the app; they can change by location.
  • Bring your QR code on your phone — rewards land in your Droppie wallet.

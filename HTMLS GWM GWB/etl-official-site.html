Energy Technology List (ETL)

The UK’s government-backed list of independently verified, energy-efficient products — around 8,000 listings representing the top 25% most efficient in their class.

How the ETL can help

Use the list to find verified high-efficiency products by technology category, with impartial purchaser information that supports Net Zero buying decisions.

It is not a shop and not a Greenways catalogue — it is the UK government scheme’s product database and purchaser guidance.

What “listed” means

Listed products meet published energy-performance criteria for their technology category. Listing is not a payback figure, cost estimate, or tax-relief calculation.

Example technology note

Heat recovery ventilation (HRV) is a separate ETL category from boilers, heat pumps, and general HVAC. Product eligibility and category names come from the ETL product database.

Greenways vs the official list

The Greenways marketplace may show energy-efficient equipment examples that map to ETL product IDs for listing status and specifications. Those rows help you cross-check against the UK list.

Narrative payback, grant totals, and £/€ ranges on Greenways pages are illustrative Greenways modelling or other published sources — not ETL tax relief and not ETL-published savings.

Energy Technology List (ETL)

UK Government-Backed Energy Efficiency with European Impact

The Energy Technology List (ETL) is one of the world's largest databases of energy-saving technology, featuring over 8,000 independently verified and accredited energy efficient products across 21 technology categories, backed by the UK Government's Department for Energy Security and Net Zero (DESNZ). While UK-based, ETL-listed products are internationally recognized and widely used across Europe, supporting the continent's transition to Net Zero.

What Makes the ETL Special

Free & Impartial

Completely free to use for both purchasers and manufacturers, with government-backed impartial verification and transparent criteria.

Stringent Assessment

All products undergo rigorous independent testing and must represent the top 25% most energy efficient products in their market class.

Always Current

Regularly updated and aligned with government best practice, with products that no longer meet criteria being removed.

Net Zero Support

Directly supports the UK's Net Zero journey and helps businesses save on running costs during the energy crisis.

Who Uses ETL Products

🚇 London Underground

Transport for London uses ETL criteria to ensure all electrical and mechanical equipment meets the highest energy efficiency standards across the network.

🎓 Universities

The University of Reading and other educational institutions use ETL for procuring high-efficiency hand dryers, boilers, and refrigeration units.

🍽️ Foodservice Industry

The Foodservice Equipment Association (FEA) collaborates with ETL to help businesses identify energy-saving kitchen equipment.

🏢 Public Sector

Government Buying Standards specify ETL for procurement purposes, ensuring public money supports the most efficient technologies.

🏭 Manufacturing

Industrial facilities across the UK and Europe use ETL-listed motors, compressors, and HVAC systems for optimal efficiency.

🌍 European Businesses

Many ETL-listed products are manufactured by European companies and exported globally, supporting continent-wide efficiency goals.

Technology Categories Available

🔥 Heating Systems

Boiler equipment, heat pumps, combined heat and power (CHP), and radiant heaters with exceptional efficiency ratings.

❄️ Cooling & Refrigeration

Professional refrigeration equipment and HVAC systems that minimize energy consumption while maintaining performance.

⚡ Motors & Electrical

High-efficiency motors, drives, fans, and uninterruptible power supplies (UPS) for industrial applications.

💡 Lighting & Controls

Advanced lighting systems and energy monitoring equipment that reduce consumption and improve control.

♻️ Recovery Systems

Heat recovery ventilation, wastewater heat recovery, and waste heat to electricity conversion equipment.

☀️ Renewable Energy

Solar thermal collectors, energy storage systems, and other renewable energy technologies.

European Impact

While the ETL is UK-managed, many listed products are manufactured by European companies and meet or exceed EU energy efficiency standards. These technologies support the European Green Deal objectives and are widely adopted across European markets, contributing to continental Net Zero targets and energy security goals.
